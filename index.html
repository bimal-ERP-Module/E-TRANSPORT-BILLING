Customer Rate Master

Customers load automatically from GitHub. Starter sample rates are not used for billing until approved; the imported rate is used instead. Edit and save each real rate to approve it. Saving a rate increase or decrease updates all current trips for that customer and their billing totals immediately. Original imported amounts and saved bill snapshots are retained. One current fixed rate per customer is supported.

Saved Bills

Vendor summaries show PAN, trips, quantity and total billing amount. Saving the same trips again updates their saved bill. Unpriced trips are retained and clearly identified; their amounts are excluded from the total. Saved snapshots retain their amounts. Use the same browser and site to keep saved bills.

Vendor Payments

Vendor-wise transport amount from the current trips (approved rate, otherwise imported rate). Click a vendor to see its trips. Press Save Vendor Data inside a vendor to send it to the Payment Tracker.

Payment Tracker

Shows your saved transport bills: vendor, saved transport amount, payment done and closing pending. Save a vendor's data from Vendor Payments (Save Vendor Data) or Transport Billing (Save Bill) to see it here. Example: transport amount 20,000, paid 15,000 → closing pending 5,000. Every payment is saved with its date. Use the same browser and site to keep payments.
